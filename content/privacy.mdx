## Project enquiries

The enquiry form sends the information you enter to Web3Forms for email delivery. It is used to respond to your enquiry. Please avoid including passwords, payment details or sensitive customer records.

Web3Forms delivers enquiries to the email address registered to this site’s access key. You can also email [sudipta1604129@gmail.com](mailto:sudipta1604129@gmail.com) directly. Ask by email if you would like your enquiry information removed.

## Site analytics

On Vercel deployments, the site integrates Vercel Web Analytics to understand visits. If enabled and supported by the project’s plan, it records a successful enquiry event with the selected service category. Names, email addresses and project descriptions are not included in that event.

It also records non-identifying actions such as opening a project, article, resume or repository link. These events use public project/article slugs or action labels, without enquiry details or visitor-entered text.

[Vercel’s analytics privacy information](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy) and [Web3Forms’ privacy policy](https://web3forms.com/privacy) explain their respective services.

## Your theme preference

The dark or light mode selection is saved in your browser’s local storage so the site remembers your preference. This preference is not sent with your enquiry.
